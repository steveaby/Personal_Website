01 // OVERVIEW_&_MOTIVATION

University dining halls prepare thousands of meals daily, routinely generating prepared food surplus that goes to waste due to strict end-of-service discard policies. Concurrently, many college students experience food insecurity or struggle to find affordable, fresh meals between classes.

ReServe bridges this disconnect by providing an automated, real-time surplus food marketplace. Dining hall staff and university organizations can publish surplus meal batches in seconds with strict pickup windows, while students can browse, filter by dietary requirements, and instantly reserve meals before discard times.

02 // SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE_&_DATA_FLOW

ReServe is architected as a modern full-stack web platform with strict role isolation:

  • Frontend (React 19 & Vite 7): Single-page application utilizing modular CSS, custom state stores, and dynamic client-side filtering for zero-latency search across active meal listings.
  • Backend REST API (Node.js ESM & Express 5): Handles business logic, transactional meal claims, bcrypt password hashing, JWT session signing, and Google OAuth 2.0 integration.
  • Data Persistence (MongoDB Atlas & Mongoose 8): Document database modeling Users, Listings, Reservations, and Analytics with compound indexes for fast geospatial and dietary queries.
  • Automated Expiration Background Cron: An automated server-side scheduler executes periodically to mark listings as expired the instant their pickup window closes, preventing students from reserving outdated food.
03 // CORE_ENGINEERING_HIGHLIGHTS
  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Strict route middleware separates Student Buyers (marketplace discovery, reservation claiming, order history) from Dining Staff Sellers (surplus posting, inventory decrementing, donation impact logs).
  • Real-Time Environmental Impact Tracking: Mathematically aggregates meals rescued, estimated food weight diverted, and CO₂ equivalent greenhouse emissions prevented across the entire campus.
  • Dietary & Allergen Filtering Matrix: Instant multi-tag matching supporting Vegan, Vegetarian, Halal, Gluten-Free, and High-Protein diets with UI warning badges.
  • Secure Dual Authentication: Supports standard email/password authentication with salted bcrypt hashing alongside one-click Google OAuth 2.0 token verification.
